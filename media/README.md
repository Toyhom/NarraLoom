# NarraLoom media

The [illustrated tour](../guides/features.md) presents the reference frontend in English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. Screenshots show sample worlds and stories; their authored content can use a different language from the interface.

The video soundtrack, **Threads of a World**, is an original instrumental composition for NarraLoom. Original screenshots, captions and music are provided under the repository's [MIT license](../LICENSE). The optional portrait uses the [Roleplay Avatar integration](../guides/reference/avatars.md); upstream code and resource terms are listed in [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md).

Watch the localized videos in the [English](../README.md), [Chinese](../README.zh-CN.md) or [Japanese](../README.ja.md) README. Each tour lasts 4 minutes 10 seconds. Its chapter positions are listed in the corresponding illustrated guide.

Download the on-screen captions: [English](captions/en.vtt) · [简体中文](captions/zh-CN.vtt) · [日本語](captions/ja.vtt).
